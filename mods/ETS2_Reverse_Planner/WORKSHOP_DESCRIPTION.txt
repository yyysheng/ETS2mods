[h1]English[/h1]
ReversePlanner identifies the active game-native task parking frame and continuously plans a reverse route for the attached trailer. It draws two green tracks following the tractor's rear corners: the first 7 m is solid and the remaining planned route is dashed. Steering arrows beside the cab update continuously.

[b]Features in v0.1.0-beta[/b]
[list]
[*]Guides an attached trailer to the unique active task parking frame.
[*]Uses measured tractor wheelbase, fifth-wheel offset, trailer axle geometry, lift state and steerable axle angle.
[*]Shows smooth left/right/reverse arrows beside the cab and a red X only after route failure persists for one second.
[*]Uses a 70-degree articulation hard limit and validates tractor/trailer contours.
[*]Runs beside Reverse Posture Assistant v0.10.10 through a cooperative hook sidecar.
[*]Uses no ReShade add-on, camera capture, mirror capture, depth buffer or graphics proxy DLL.
[/list]

[b]Beta notice[/b]
This is a driver aid, not autonomous driving. Detached tractor-to-trailer guidance is intentionally disabled. General traffic and building perception is incomplete, so a green route is not a collision-free guarantee.

[h1]中文[/h1]
ReversePlanner 会识别当前任务的游戏原生停车框，并为已连接挂车持续规划倒车路线。两条绿色轨迹跟随车头尾部左右角：前 7 米为实线，后续完整推算路线为虚线；车头两侧方向箭头持续更新。

[b]v0.1.0-beta 功能[/b]
[list]
[*]仅引导已连接挂车进入唯一活动任务停车框。
[*]根据实际车头轴距、鞍座偏移、挂车轴组、提升状态和可转向轴角度进行适配。
[*]车头两侧显示平滑左转、右转或继续倒车箭头；持续无解一秒后才显示红色 X。
[*]使用 70 度铰接硬限制，并检查车头与挂车自身轮廓。
[*]通过协作式 Hook 与 Reverse Posture Assistant v0.10.10 共存。
[*]不使用 ReShade Add-on，不读取摄像头、后视镜、深度缓冲，也不安装图形代理 DLL。
[/list]
