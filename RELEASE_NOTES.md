# ETS2 Reverse Posture Assistant v0.6.0

First public release for Euro Truck Simulator 2 1.60.

## Highlights

- Live tractor and connected-trailer reverse posture prediction.
- 2D road, yard, and terrain outlines within a performance-focused 30 m range.
- Automatic display on 14 original infotainment layouts while reverse gear is selected.
- No forced camera switching and no mirror-camera reuse.
- Full standalone installer and a runtime-only package for Steam Workshop subscribers.

## Downloads

- `ETS2_Reverse_Posture_Assistant_v0.6.0_Full.zip`: complete installation including the `.scs` mod and required runtime.
- `ETS2_Reverse_Posture_Assistant_v0.6.0_Runtime_for_Workshop.zip`: runtime components only; use this after subscribing on Steam Workshop.

## 中文

这是适用于 Euro Truck Simulator 2 1.60 的首个公开版本。挂入倒挡后，支持的原版娱乐屏会显示车头与挂车未来姿态，并叠加 30 m 范围内的道路、货场和地形轮廓。该版本不会切换玩家视角，也不会调用前视镜、后视镜或摄像机画面。

- 独立安装请选择 `Full` 包。
- 通过 Steam 创意工坊订阅后请选择 `Runtime_for_Workshop` 包。
